Raisin Necter Cake

by Vera

A vintage nectar cake, referred to as a "war cake" from those who lived through the second World War during food shortages. This cake turns simple ingredients into a heartwarming dessert.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 Cup Rasins
  • 1 Cup Hot Water
  • 3/4 Cup White or Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 Cup Butter or Butter Substitute
  • 1 Egg, Well Beaten
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Vanilla Flavoring or Extract
  • 1 2/3 Cups Flour
  • 1/2 Cup Chopped Nuts(Optional)
  • 1 Teaspoon Nutmeg
  • 1 Teaspoon Cinnamon
  • 1 Teaspoon Allspice
  • 1 Teaspoon Baking-Soda
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Salt

Directions

  1. In a medium pot combine raisins and water. Bring to a boil and keep on heat for 10 minutes. Save 3/4 cup of the boiled water and drain the rest. set raisins and water aside.
  2. Preheat oven to 375° F (230° C).
  3. In a large bowl cream butter/substitute and suger. Add egg and mix until incorporated.
  4. Sift flour, baking soda, salt and spices into the bowl, folding as you go.
  5. Add the set aside raisins, raisin water, vanilla and nuts. Beat thoroughly.
  6. Pour batter into oiled cake pan. Bake for 30 minutes or until fork-dry.
  7. Garnish the cake with our cream cheese frosting for best result.

Tasty and simple cake, the family could't get enough of it!
